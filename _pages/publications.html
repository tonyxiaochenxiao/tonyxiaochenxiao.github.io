Robust Motif Discovery Using Topological Data Analysis

Joint with Baris Coskunuzer (The University of Texas at Dallas), Kenneth McCabe (Northeastern University), and Jose A. Perea (Northeastern University).
In preparation. Target: ACM ICMR 2027.
We develop a method for identifying recurring melodic motifs in symbolic music under transposition, rhythmic variation, and ornamentation, using persistent homology of multiscale phrase-recurrence structures to separate genuine repetitions from accidental similarities; benchmarked on BPS-Motif (Beethoven piano sonatas).
